About ZaxEd✕

    

      
ZaxEd 0.1 - map viewer for Zax: The Alien Hunter.

      
Reads the game's DATA.dat directly; nothing is modified.

      
Drag with the hand tool, middle or right mouse button to pan; mouse wheel to zoom;
        click objects to inspect them; double-click opens a properties window. Sprites show each
        object's first frame.
